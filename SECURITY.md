# Security reporting

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to info@westwoodrobotics.io. Put
`SECURITY` and `BEAR Calibration Tool` in the subject. Include the application
version, operating-system version, affected BEAR product and firmware, expected
behavior, observed behavior, and reproducible steps. Do not send secrets,
personal data, or a complete device Flash image unless Westwood Robotics
specifically requests it through a protected channel.

Westwood Robotics Corporation may review and respond to reports at its
discretion. This channel does not create a promised response time, remediation
deadline, support period, update schedule, or obligation to continue providing
the Software. Ordinary correspondence and voluntarily submitted logs are
normally deleted or anonymized within one year after the last correspondence,
subject to legal, safety, security, and claims-preservation needs.
